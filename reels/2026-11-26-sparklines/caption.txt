Who's slipping? One tiny chart per row tells you in a second 📈

=SPARKLINE(B2:F2) draws a trend line inside the cell (Google Sheets). Fill it down for every row.
=SPARKLINE(B3:F3,{"color","red"}) colors one line red to flag it.
Excel: Insert > Sparklines > Line, pick the data cells, OK, then drag the handle down.

Works in Google Sheets, Excel 2016+ (Windows) and Excel 2021+ / Microsoft 365 (Mac). In Excel it's a menu feature, not a formula.
