Biggest number on top? SORT does it, but Excel and Google Sheets spell "descending" differently.

Excel: =SORT(A2:B6,2,-1) → sorts by column 2, high to low
Google Sheets: =SORT(A2:B6,2,FALSE) → same result
Low to high: 1 in Excel, TRUE in Sheets

Works in Excel 365 / 2021 / 2024 and Google Sheets (Sheets needs the column and order arguments).
