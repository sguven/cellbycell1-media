=COUNTIF(C2:C8,">60000") checks one rule, so Ops reps get counted too.
=COUNTIFS(B2:B8,"Sales",C2:C8,">60000") counts a row only when BOTH rules match.
Pattern: range, rule, range, rule. Keep rules like ">60000" in quotes.
