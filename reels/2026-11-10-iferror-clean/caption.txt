Hide those ugly DIV/0! errors with one wrapper 🙈

=IFERROR(B2/C2, "")  →  shows the price, or a blank cell when Units is 0 or empty.
=IFERROR(B2/C2, "No units")  →  show a message (or 0, or a dash) instead of the error.
It catches every error type (DIV/0!, N/A, VALUE!, REF!) and typos like NAME? too. Fix real mistakes first, then wrap the ones you expect.

Works in Excel (365, 2016 and newer, web, Mac) and Google Sheets. Excel needs both arguments; in Sheets the second one is optional (blank if left out).
