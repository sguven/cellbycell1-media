Copied your formula down and got $0.00 everywhere? One key fixes it.

=B2*D2 → copied down, D2 slides to D3, D4... (empty cells = 0)
=B2*$D$2 → the $ signs lock the rate cell, every row is right
Keep pressing F4 to cycle: $D$2 → D$2 → $D2 → D2

Works in Excel (Windows: F4 · Mac: Cmd+T) and Google Sheets (F4 · Mac: Fn+F4), while editing the formula.
