Stop adding sales by hand ✋ One formula does it with 2 conditions.

=SUMIFS(C2:C7, A2:A7, "Maya", B2:B7, "Jan")  →  adds Sales (C) only where Rep = Maya AND Month = Jan.
Order: sum range FIRST, then range/criteria pairs. Add more pairs for more conditions.
Watch out: SUMIF flips it. =SUMIF(A2:A7, "Maya", C2:C7) puts the sum range LAST.
Text criteria go in "quotes".

Works in Excel (365, 2016 and newer, web, Mac) and Google Sheets, same syntax in both.
