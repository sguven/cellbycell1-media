Stop gluing cells together with & 🙅

=TEXTJOIN(", ", TRUE, A2:A7) turns a whole column into one cell: Ava, Ben, Cara, Dev, Eli, Fay
", " = what goes between each item (try " / " or " - ")
TRUE = skip empty cells (FALSE keeps them and leaves ", ," gaps)
Bonus: =TEXTJOIN(", ", TRUE, FILTER(A2:A7, B2:B7="Sales")) lists only the Sales team

Works in Excel 365, 2024, 2021, 2019 and Google Sheets (the FILTER combo needs Excel 365/2021+ or Sheets).
