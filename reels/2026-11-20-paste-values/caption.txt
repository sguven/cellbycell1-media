Pasted your totals and every cell broke? Ctrl+V pasted the formula, not the numbers.

Google Sheets: Ctrl+Shift+V → paste values only (Cmd+Shift+V on Mac)
Excel (Windows): Ctrl+Alt+V → V → Enter (Paste Special › Values)
You get plain numbers: no formulas, no broken references.

Works in Google Sheets and Excel for Windows.
