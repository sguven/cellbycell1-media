Tick a box and the task crosses itself out ✅ No macros, just 1 rule.

1. Select A2:A6 > Insert > Checkbox (checked = TRUE, empty = FALSE)
2. Select B2:C6 and add a conditional formatting rule with the formula =$A2=TRUE, then pick Strikethrough
Excel: Home > Conditional Formatting > New Rule > Use a formula > Format > Font
Google Sheets: Format > Conditional formatting > Custom formula is
Bonus: =COUNTIF(A2:A6,TRUE) counts what's done.

Works in Google Sheets and Excel for Microsoft 365 (cell checkboxes are a Microsoft 365 feature).
