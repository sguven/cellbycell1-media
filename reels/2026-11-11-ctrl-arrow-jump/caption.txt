Stop scrolling to find the last row of your data 🛑

Ctrl + Down Arrow jumps straight to the last filled cell in the column (row 5,000 in one keystroke).
Ctrl + Shift + Down Arrow selects everything from your cell down to the end.
Works with every arrow: Up, Down, Left, Right.
Heads up: a blank cell stops the jump, so press again to keep going.

Works in Excel and Google Sheets. On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl.
