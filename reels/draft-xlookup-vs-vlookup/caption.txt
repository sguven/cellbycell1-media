STOP using VLOOKUP. Here's why 👇

1. It can't look left
2. Insert a column and =VLOOKUP(...,3,FALSE) returns the wrong column
3. Forget FALSE and it returns a wrong match with no error

The fix: =XLOOKUP(what, search_range, return_range)
Looks left, exact match by default, and add "Not found" as a 4th argument.
Works in Excel 365 / 2021+ and Google Sheets.
