Still typing "Done" by hand? Make a dropdown in 10 seconds 👇

Excel: select the cells > Data > Data Validation > Allow: List > Source: Done,In progress,To do
Google Sheets: Insert > Dropdown (or right-click > Dropdown), add your items and pick a color for each
Everyone picks from the list, so the spelling is the same every time and filters stay accurate

Works in Excel 2016+ and Microsoft 365 (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets.
