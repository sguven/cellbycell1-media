Stop copy-pasting filtered rows. One formula does it live 👇

=FILTER(A2:C7, B2:B7="Sales") pulls every Sales row into a new table that spills down by itself.
Change the data and the result updates instantly, no clicks.
Two conditions? Multiply them: =FILTER(A2:C7, (B2:B7="Sales")*(C2:C7>1000))
No matches? Excel: add a 3rd argument like "None". Sheets: wrap it in IFNA(..., "None").

Works in Excel 365 / 2021 / 2024 and Google Sheets (not Excel 2019 or older). Keep the cells below empty so it can spill.
