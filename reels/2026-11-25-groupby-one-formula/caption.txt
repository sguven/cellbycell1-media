A pivot table in ONE formula? Yes, in Excel 365.

=GROUPBY(A2:A9,B2:B9,SUM,,0,-2)
A2:A9 = what to group by · B2:B9 = what to add · SUM = how to combine
0 = no total row · -2 = sort by column 2, biggest first

Works in Excel for Microsoft 365. Google Sheets: =QUERY(A1:B9,"select A, sum(B) group by A order by sum(B) desc",1) · Older Excel: use a PivotTable.
