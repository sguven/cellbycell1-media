Duplicate rows are wrecking your totals. Delete them in seconds 👇

Excel: select your data > Data tab > Remove Duplicates > tick the columns to check > OK
Google Sheets: Data > Data cleanup > Remove duplicates
Heads up: it deletes whole rows, so keep a copy first (Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z undoes it).
Want to keep the original? =UNIQUE(A2:A7) lists each value once in a new column.

Remove Duplicates works in Excel (Windows & Mac) and Google Sheets. UNIQUE needs Excel 365 / 2021+ or Google Sheets.
