Your "30-day" deadline is really 23 work days 📅

=C2-B2 counts calendar days, weekends included
=NETWORKDAYS(B2,C2) counts Mon–Fri only (start and end day both count)
=NETWORKDAYS(B2,C2,F2:F4) also skips the holiday dates in F2:F4

Works in Excel 2007+ / Microsoft 365 and Google Sheets. Different weekend days? Use NETWORKDAYS.INTL.
