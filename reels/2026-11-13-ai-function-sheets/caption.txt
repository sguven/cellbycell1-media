There's an AI formula hiding in Google Sheets, and it reads your text for you 👇

=AI("Positive, negative or neutral?", A2)
Type it, select the cell, click Generate and Insert, then fill it down to tag every row.
Change the prompt for other jobs: "Summarize in 5 words", "Write a polite reply", "Price, quality or shipping?"
Heads up: answers are text only, they don't update on their own (click Refresh), it can't be nested inside IF(), and AI can be wrong, so spot-check.

Google Sheets on a computer, with an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Excel's =COPILOT() function was retired in Sept 2026: ask the Copilot pane instead.
