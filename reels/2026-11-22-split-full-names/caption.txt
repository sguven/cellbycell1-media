Still splitting names by hand? Type ONE first name and let the sheet fill in the rest ✂️

Excel: type the first name next to the full name, press Ctrl + E (Flash Fill)
Repeat for the last name, or use Data > Flash Fill
Google Sheets: =SPLIT(A2, " ") puts first and last in two cells, then fill down

Works in Excel 2016+ / Microsoft 365 (Flash Fill) and Google Sheets (SPLIT). Tip: middle names give you a 3rd column with SPLIT.
