Scrolled to row 50 and forgot which column is which? Freeze the header row 👇

Excel: View > Freeze Panes > Freeze Top Row (Mac: View > Freeze Top Row)
Google Sheets: View > Freeze > 1 row
Row 1 now stays on top while you scroll. Undo: Unfreeze Panes (Excel) or Freeze > No rows (Sheets)

Works in Excel for Microsoft 365 (Windows, Mac, web) and Google Sheets.
