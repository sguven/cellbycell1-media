10 orders, but only 6 customers. COUNTA can't tell the difference 👀

=COUNTA(UNIQUE(A2:A11)) → counts each name once
UNIQUE drops the repeats, COUNTA counts what's left
Use an exact range with no blank cells

Works in Excel 365 / 2021+ and Google Sheets (Sheets also has =COUNTUNIQUE(A2:A11)).
